# Sovereign Dispute Engine — Console

The credit-repair algorithm, run as a campaign. A credit report is
ingested; every tradeline field is audited against the reporting
standard (Metro 2) and the bureau's accuracy duty under the FCRA.
Fields that cannot be proven conforming are **marked** — and a marked
field is a field the next letter attacks.

Rounds escalate the way the letters always did: one bureau letter
carrying every failure, then a letter per tradeline naming its exact
fields, then the same case served on the bureau and the furnisher
together, then the full barrage — every failure its own letter.
Between rounds, the fresh report is audited again. What survived is
the feedback; what is deleted is the verdict landing.

Inquiries run on the second front: an inquiry with no documented
permissible purpose under FCRA § 604 is challenged on its own —
remove it, or produce physical proof of the purpose.

Every round seals into a hash-chained verdict log. The bureau never
grades its own record. The audit does.
